# POX-BOY 3026 - Idea Backlog

This document serves as a persistent tracker for features and mechanics that have been discussed but are **not yet implemented** in the active codebase.

## 1. Faction Leader / Overseer Authentication
*   **Concept:** Create a hidden "Overseer Mode" (e.g., accessed by tapping the app logo 5 times and entering a master PIN).
*   **Mechanic:** This unlocks a "Vendor/Admin UI" that allows authorized event staff and Faction Leaders to generate infinite Item/Quest QR codes to hand out to attendees, without needing those items in their own inventory.
*   **Restriction:** Regular attendees would have their ability to freely spawn items revoked, requiring them to physically interact with Faction Leaders or scavenge QR codes hidden around the venue to gain loot.

## 2. Peer-to-Peer Map Sharing (Static Player Tracking)
*   **Concept:** Since live peer-to-peer tracking requires a costly backend server, implement an offline P2P location sharing mechanic instead.
*   **Mechanic:** A player can click a button to generate a QR code of their *exact current GPS coordinates*. When another player scans it, it drops a permanent, named waypoint on the scanner's map (e.g., "Scrap-Iron's Last Known Location").

## 3. High-Volume Radio Compression
*   **Concept:** Load the app with hours of local music for the `RADIO` tab without bloating the offline PWA cache size (which causes slow load times at the gate).
*   **Mechanic:** Compress a massive library of `.mp3` or `.ogg` files down to ~64kbps mono. This heavily reduces file size and acts as a diegetic "lo-fi wasteland radio" filter.

## 4. Live Internet Radio Integration
*   **Concept:** If a live Icecast/Shoutcast server is running at the event, plug the streaming URL directly into the Pox-Boy `RADIO` tab.
*   **Mechanic:** Replaces the static local audio files with a live stream, allowing an actual DJ to broadcast to all attendee Pip-Boys simultaneously (requires cell service/wifi at the venue).

## 5. Pox-Boy Photo Mode
*   **Concept:** Add a dedicated "Camera" tab to the interface.
*   **Mechanic:** Opens the device camera and applies heavy CSS/Canvas filters (CRT scanlines, monochrome green tint, heavy contrast) over the live feed. A shutter button allows the user to snap and save a stylized wasteland selfie directly to their phone's camera roll.

## 6. Dynamic/Earned Field Traits
*   **Concept:** Allow users to earn permanent new Traits or Feats based on things they do, see, or survive during the live event (e.g., "Witnessed the War Rig Crash", "Survived the Thunderdome", "Guzzoline Champion").
*   **Mechanic:** These earned traits would be listed alongside their starting trait on the `STAT` screen, serving as a digital badge of honor/souvenir for their roleplay achievements.
*   **Implementation:** These would likely be awarded via an "Overseer Mode" override, where authorized staff can manually inject or scan a secure QR code to permanently bind the new trait to the player's profile.

## 7. Expanded Overseer Mode (Live RP Engine)
*   **Concept:** Evolve the Overseer Mode from just a "Vendor UI" into a full Live-Action Roleplay (LARP) Game Master tool.
*   **Mechanic:** A hidden menu where admins can not only distribute items and quests, but forcefully edit a player's core stats on the fly (e.g., permanently reducing someone's Max HP if they get "injured" in a storyline, instantly changing their faction allegiances, or altering their Pox-Boy screen color to signify a radioactive mutation). 

## 8. Secure Economy & Item Locking
*   **Concept:** Prevent attendees from freely adding real-world value items (like Drink Tickets, Meal Vouchers, or rare gear) to their own inventory.
*   **Mechanic:** Remove the `+ ADD ONE` button from the standard user interface. Attendees can only *consume* items or *trade* them. To gain new items, they must scan an official QR code generated by an Overseer or Faction Leader.
*   **Alternative Mechanic:** Keep the `+ ADD ONE` button, but clicking it triggers the `OVERSEER AUTHORIZATION` modal, requiring an admin PIN to successfully duplicate or grant the item.

## 9. The "Wastelander Rolodex" (Player-to-Player Scanning)
*   **Concept:** A social log (similar to the Locations Discovered list) that tracks other attendees the user has physically met and interacted with.
*   **Mechanic:** Users can generate a "Profile QR" code. When another player scans it, the app takes a snapshot of their stats, traits, feats, and current faction alignment, saving it permanently into a new `WASTELANDERS MET` sub-tab. 
*   **UI:** Clicking a name in this Rolodex opens a neat, formatted profile card displaying their stats and loyalties.

## 10. The Bounty Hunting System (PVP Quests)
*   **Concept:** A system where players can place, share, and collect bounties on specific attendees.
*   **Prerequisite:** A player must exist in the user's "Wastelander Rolodex" (previously scanned) before a bounty can be issued against them.
*   **Mechanic:** An Overseer (or player) generates a "Bounty Quest" targeting a specific user ID. To "complete" this quest, the bounty hunter must physically track down that specific attendee and successfully scan their "Profile QR" code again to verify the capture, which then clears the quest and awards the reputation/caps.

## 11. Dynamic Bounty Rewards & Progression
*   **Concept:** Successfully completing a PVP Bounty Quest should yield tangible rewards beyond just Faction Reputation.
*   **Mechanic (Loot):** Completing a bounty automatically injects a specific reward into the hunter's inventory (e.g., "Bounty Caps", "Guzzoline Tickets", or unique gear specified by the Overseer who issued the bounty).
*   **Mechanic (Progression Trait):** Add a leveling "Bounty Hunter" trait/feat to the player's profile. Every time they successfully scan a capture, an internal counter increments. At certain thresholds (e.g., 3 bounties, 10 bounties), the trait levels up (e.g., Novice Tracker -> Elite Regulator), potentially unlocking new titles or bonuses on their `STAT` screen.

## 12. In-App Photo Gallery
*   **Concept:** A way to view the photos taken by the Pox-Boy camera directly inside the app, rather than having to leave the app and check the phone's native camera roll.
*   **Mechanic:** Photos taken and "saved" inside the `CAM` tab are written into `localStorage` (or `IndexedDB` if files get too large). A new button `[VIEW ARCHIVE]` on the `CAM` tab opens a gallery modal where users can scroll through their stylized wasteland photos.

## 13. Landscape UX Optimization (Map & Camera)
*   **The Problem:** When running in landscape orientation on a mobile device, the vertical height is severely limited. The map canvas and the camera viewfinder get squished, making them difficult to interact with.
*   **Solution A (Dynamic Resizing):** Use a `ResizeObserver` or CSS `calc()` logic. When the user navigates to the `MAP` or `CAM` tab, the UI detects the orientation and automatically hides non-essential headers/footers (like the Pip-Boy 3026 OS title and the Clock) to dedicate 100% of the available vertical real estate to the viewport.
*   **Solution B (Dedicated Fullscreen Modes):** Add a specific `[EXPAND VIEW]` button directly inside the Map and Camera tabs. Clicking it creates a temporary overlay that completely covers the standard Pip-Boy UI, showing only the map/camera and a `[BACK]` button. 

## 14. UI Layout Restructure (Landscape Optimization)
*   **Concept:** The standard vertical-stack layout (Header -> Tabs -> Content -> Footer) is too cramped for landscape phone screens, specifically for the Map and Camera.
*   **Action 1 (Remove Footer):** Completely delete the global footer (`[HP]`, `[CAPS]`, etc.). Move any remaining functional buttons (like `+ ADD ITEM` or `[DEV] RESET`) directly into the content area of their respective tabs.
*   **Action 2 (Asymmetric Split-Screen):** Redesign the `MAP` and `CAM` tabs to use a 2/3 vs 1/3 layout. 
    *   **Left Side (66%):** The live map canvas or camera viewfinder takes up the massive left block.
    *   **Right Side (33%):** All controls (GPS toggle, add marker, take photo, view gallery) are stacked vertically in a control panel on the right.
